About Rankly.ai — Transforming Enterprise Hiring with Ethical AI

Rankly.ai was founded on a simple truth: Hiring top talent shouldn't take weeks of manual keyword skimming, and candidates deserve unbiased, transparent evaluation of their true skills.

Explainable, Non-Black-Box AI

Every candidate score and match verdict comes with deterministic, human-readable rationale. Recruiters understand exactly why a candidate qualified or fell short.

Human-in-the-Loop Safeguards

AI assists, summarizes, and screens, but final employment decisions remain under the authoritative command of human HR professionals.

Strict Data Sovereignty

We never sell or train third-party public foundation models on private applicant resume data. Your talent data belongs 100% to your enterprise.
