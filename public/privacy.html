Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026 • Effective Date: Immediate

Our Core Privacy Promise

Rankly.ai never sells your resume data or contact details to third parties. Uploaded documents are processed with zero-retention foundation APIs and are never used to train public AI models.

01

Introduction & Scope

Rankly.ai ("we", "our", or "us") operates an enterprise-grade AI Applicant Tracking System (ATS) and resume screening intelligence platform. This Privacy Policy details the policies and practices regarding the collection, use, processing, and protection of personal data when you interact with our platform, website, and automated evaluation services.

02

Information We Collect

We collect information to provide intelligent resume screening and hiring pipeline management:

Account Credentials

Name, email, phone number, encrypted password hash, and OAuth authentication tokens.

Resume & Portfolio Files

Uploaded PDF, DOCX, and TXT documents, skills, work history, and educational qualifications.

Screening & Match Scores

AI match percentages, skill breakdown matrices, interview notes, and pipeline stages.

Technical Diagnostics

Anonymized IP addresses, browser agents, and self-healing telemetry logs for platform stability.

03

AI Processing & Model Training Standards

Rankly.ai integrates enterprise inference providers (including Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, and DeepSeek) to match candidates against job descriptions.

🔒 Enterprise Zero-Retention LLM Processing

All AI screening requests are transmitted via encrypted API channels under zero-data-retention agreements. Your resumes are never used to train, fine-tune, or improve public foundational AI models.

04

How We Protect & Secure Your Data

  • In-Transit Encryption: Enforced HTTPS/TLS 1.3 protocol across all API endpoints and web views.
  • Cryptographic Password Security: Standard Bcrypt hashing with 10-salt rounds before database storage.
  • Session Isolation: Secure SQLite session stores with HttpOnly, SameSite cookie flags and cloud proxy defense.
  • Self-Healing Anomaly Monitoring: Background 2-minute health checker protecting against memory leaks and unauthorized intrusion attempts.
05

Your Data Rights (GDPR & DPDP Act 2023)

Under GDPR, CCPA, and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you have full control over your data:

Right to Access & Portability: Request full export of your evaluations and profile data in JSON format.
Right to Erasure (Deletion): Request permanent purging of your account and uploaded resumes at any time.
Right to Rectification: Update or rectify outdated skills, contact details, or experience history.
Right to Withdraw Consent: Opt-out of candidate matching notifications and automated emails.
