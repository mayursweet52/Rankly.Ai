Privacy Policy for Rankly.ai

Last updated: August 25, 2026 • Effective Date: Immediate

1 Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Rankly.ai ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and protecting the confidentiality of your personal, employment, and recruitment data. Rankly.ai is an intelligent applicant tracking and AI resume screening platform designed to assist job seekers and talent acquisition teams with merit-based candidate evaluation.

This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect, how we process and store resume documents, our use of generative AI models, and your legal rights regarding your data under applicable data privacy regulations (including GDPR, CCPA, and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023).

2 Information We Collect

When you use Rankly.ai, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, encrypted passwords, profession, and OAuth credentials (Google/LinkedIn login).
  • Resume & Professional Documents: Text, work history, skill sets, educational qualifications, projects, certifications, and files uploaded in PDF, DOCX, or TXT format.
  • Evaluation & Screening Records: AI Match Scores, skill gap analytics, fit verdicts, hiring manager feedback notes, and pipeline status.
  • Feedback & Communications: Messages, ratings, and inquiries submitted through our feedback widget or support channels.
  • Technical Telemetry: Anonymized IP addresses, browser types, session IDs, and error telemetry logs used strictly for security monitoring and system health diagnostics.

3 How We Use AI & Process Resume Data

Rankly.ai utilizes enterprise AI inference engines (such as Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, and DeepSeek) to analyze candidate profiles against job descriptions.

Zero Public Model Training

Your resume documents and candidate details are processed via encrypted API calls with zero-retention policies. Your private resume data is never used to train public foundation models.

  • Evaluating skills alignment against target job requirements.
  • Extracting structured profile summaries and missing skill recommendations.
  • Generating personalized interview questions and career counseling guidance.
  • Providing ATS pipeline management for authorized employers and recruiters.

4 Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do NOT sell, rent, or monetize your personal data or resume files to data brokers or third-party advertisers.

Information is shared only under strict operational conditions:

  • With Your Consent: When a candidate applies to an employer or enterprise organization using Rankly.ai.
  • Essential Service Providers: Secure cloud hosting (Railway), transactional email gateways (Google Mail / Resend), and database infrastructure (Prisma SQLite).
  • Legal Obligations: If required by law, subpoena, or government authority to comply with applicable regulations.

5 Data Security & Retention

We deploy robust enterprise security safeguards to protect your personal information:

  • Encryption: All web traffic is encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3 / HTTPS (SSL) on Port 443.
  • Password Hashing: Passwords are cryptographically hashed using standard Bcrypt before storage.
  • Autonomous Telemetry & Threat Defense: Background health monitoring, rate-limiting, and anomaly detection safeguard against unauthorized access.
  • Retention Period: Resumes and account data are retained for as long as your account remains active or until you request permanent deletion.

6 Your Legal Rights (GDPR / DPDP Act)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you are entitled to the following rights:

Right to Access

Request a copy of all personal data and evaluations associated with your account.

Right to Erasure

Permanently delete your account, uploaded resumes, and scoring history at any time.

Right to Rectification

Update or correct inaccurate profile details, contact information, or preferences.

Data Portability

Export your evaluation scores and resume data in structured JSON/CSV format.
